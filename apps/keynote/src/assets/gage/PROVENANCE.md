This is **not** a photograph of Gage inside Sun labs. No rights-clear Sun-campus interior of him delivering the line was available. Commons also has a 2004 Steve Jurvetson crop ([File:John Gage.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Gage.jpg), CC BY 2.0) from TIME’s Future of Life Conference — too small (322 × 475) and not a speaking still. We use Ito’s 2008 portrait as a historically grounded still of Gage himself, attributed on the **presenter HUD**, not on the projector. The 1984 reading comes from the quote, the kicker, and the period graph — not from claiming the photo is 1984.

Do not replace this with a generated photorealistic impersonation.
